# Reuse and provenance

The dataset source is SendGuard900K v1.0.0, DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17985618, by Bobek, Nalepa, Obara, Suszalski and Bałec (2025), distributed under CC BY 4.0. Its attribution and license apply to the included data derivatives and public demo records. The raw source CSV remains at the source repository.

Original study code, protocol, fitted models, plots and reviewer tools were prepared for the associated manuscript by its authors. **No separate open-source software license has been assigned in this package.** Providing source material for review does not assign a software license or change third-party terms. The dataset's CC BY 4.0 license is not asserted as the license of all software.

Dependency packages retain their own licenses and are installed separately; their source or binaries are not vendored here. The graphics were drawn by the authors; the article used for visual comparison is not included. No logo, third-party article PDF, institutional act, signature or private application workspace is included.

`study/` is the unmodified original supplementary archive. Its own README and evidence files describe its historical scope. The enclosing README, reference figures and reviewer tools define the mapping to the submitted manuscript. `PROVENANCE.json` records the source hashes.
